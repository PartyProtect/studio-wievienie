# Studio Wievien project rule

`AGENTS.md` is the primary creative and working guide for this repository.

Use the actual material, current requirements and rendered result as the basis for decisions. Older documents may contain useful research, but their visual directions and speculative plans are not standing instructions.

Shape the canvas before choosing the art direction: understand content, purpose, hierarchy, navigation and technical constraints first. Then let the project’s own material suggest typography, color, composition, motion and interaction.

Share infrastructure when it improves reliability. Do not use shared infrastructure as a reason to make different projects look alike.

Fancy Boogers demonstrates that a project can have a distinct world inside Studio Wievien. It is a precedent for autonomy, not a style template.

When uncertain, inspect the material, make a useful experiment, render it, and judge what actually happened. Do not promote a local success into a global rule without a separate reason.
