# Studio Wievien project rule

`AGENTS.md` is the primary creative and working guide for this repository.

Use the actual Studio Wievien material and the current rendered site as the basis for decisions. Older documents in `docs/` may contain useful research, but they also contain superseded visual directions and speculative plans.

Keep the work clear, human and technically quiet. Let visual character come from Wievien's practice, typography, imagery, rhythm and deliberate interaction rather than from adding effects by default.

When uncertain, inspect what currently exists, make the smallest useful experiment, and judge the rendered result.
